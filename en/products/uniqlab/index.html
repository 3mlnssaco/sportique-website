Consented research operations

UniQLab

UniQLab.io is a workspace where researchers and institutions prepare studies and manage participants, consent and repeated data collection.

A working tool for research teams and institutions. It brings recurring documents, participant status and collaboration into one place.

What can you do?

  • Study preparation
  • Manage participant criteria
  • Review consent status
  • IRB document assistance
  • Institutional and researcher permissions
  • Data access history
  • Check visit schedules
  • Operations reports

How a study runs

UniQdataIndividual

Live

  1. 1

    The institution, in UniQLab

    Proposes a study.

    It sets the purpose, the requested items, the period and the participant criteria, and submits them.

  2. 2

    SportiQue

    Reviews the proposal.

    Only a reviewed study opens to participants.

  3. 3

    The individual, in UniQdata

    Reads the request and consents.

    They see the purpose, the requested items and the period, and choose for themselves. Consent is given study by study, and withdrawing stops any new use at once.

  4. 4

    Between the two products

    Only records within the consented scope are connected.

    An institutional account alone does not grant access to personal records.

  5. 5

    The institution, in UniQLab

    Follows participation and incoming records.

    Consent status, tasks and reminders, and incoming records are seen in one place.

  6. 6

    The individual, in UniQdata

    Keeps the records after the study ends.

    The same records can be chosen again for the next study or another use.

UniQLab and study participation in the app are in development, and the order above follows the current design.

Example use

A study with repeated check-ins

The research team defines the study period and collection fields, then runs check-ins within participants' consent. It reviews missing responses and provenance to organize study materials.

Current scope

An operations tool for research teams and institutions. Personal records are not automatically shared beyond the consented purpose and scope.

INDIVIDUAL CONTROL · BLOCKCHAIN-BASED VERIFICATION

Sharing starts with individual choice.

UniQLab.io connects records within the study purpose and scope agreed to by participants. An institutional account alone does not grant access to personal records.

SportiQue’s sharing architecture separates personal record storage, consent and permissions, and blockchain-based evidence verification. Original health records and decryption keys stay outside the public ledger.

  1. 1

    Scope chosen by the individual

    Recipients, purposes and record scope are bound to consent.

  2. 2

    Delivery bound to permissions

    The design verifies recipient permissions and keys and connects encrypted delivery to the authorized scope.

  3. 3

    Evidence separate from originals

    Evidence of consent, access and sharing is grouped into batches for blockchain verification, without publishing original records.

  4. 4

    Revocation and evidence review

    Revocation that blocks new access is separate from verification of existing history.
