The problem

Scattered records
limit personal choice.

Data remains in hospitals, screening centers, pharmacies, wearables, health apps and daily logs, but is not collected in the individual's database. Without direct control of records, individuals cannot choose where and how much to share, or which AI models and platforms to use.

Records are scattered.

Care, checkup, medication and device records remain in different formats and accounts across institutions and services.

Sharing is hard to control.

Without managing records together, it is difficult to choose the recipient, purpose and scope of sharing yourself.

Choosing AI freely is difficult, too.

When records stay within one service, it is difficult to use them with AI models or platforms outside that service.

How it works

Data with the individual.
Choice with the individual.

DataQ standardizes the formats, fields and units of health data from different sources. UniQdata.io manages records in the personal database and lets individuals choose how they are used and shared.

  1. 1

    DataQ

    Standardize formats.

    Preserve originals and provenance while connecting different formats, fields and units to a common structure.

  2. 2

    UniQdata

    Build your personal database.

    Manage health records in one place and use them over time.

  3. 3

    Choose

    Choose AI and sharing.

    Individuals choose where to use records, with which AI model or platform, for what purpose, and within what scope and duration.

What opens

Records together,
open up choices.

Individuals review their records in reports, ask AI questions and share the items caregivers need. Only the scope they consent to connects to research and purpose-specific apps.

  • Review your records in reports.

    Review time periods, sources, gaps and observed changes in the context of your personal record history.

  • Ask AI questions about your own records.

    First choose the fields, period, purpose and processing location; only the minimum approved scope is sent to AI.

  • Share only the records caregivers need.

    Choose which caregiver may access which records and for how long, and revoke access when needed.

  • Use the records you need abroad.

    Connect only authorized records where national policies and data residency requirements are met.

  • Choose the studies and records you share.

    Choose a study after reviewing its purpose and the requested fields and duration. Participation compensation is handled according to the process set by the payer, such as the institution or PI, and individuals can review the related history.

  • Connect records to care and medication apps.

    Use the same records for specific purposes, such as family care with Bodab and visit preparation with MediQ.

  • Review use history.

    Review what was used, where and why; reduce or withdraw consent and apply those decisions to subsequent use.

All use stays within the recipient, purpose, scope and duration chosen by the individual, and applicable national policy requirements.

Our story

Through family care,
a larger problem emerged.

Even when families wanted to help manage an older person's medication, activity and care, the records they needed were scattered across hospitals, pharmacies and apps.

Families had to cross-check records from several places and ask each other again to see medication confirmation and prepare for medical visits. Individuals also could not easily choose which records to share and with whom.

We decided to build beyond Bodab: a foundation where individuals bring records together, manage them and choose their use and sharing.

Record sharing,
for individuals to choose.

2025

In our 2025 XRPL demo, users selected which records to share and how long others could view them. We also implemented a flow for reviewing authorized access and use history and receiving research participation compensation automatically.

Original health records stayed in the personal database, with only permissions and use history connected. We are carrying this experience into record management and sharing choices in UniQdata.io.
